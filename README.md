## 🔬 Research focus

- **Disease prediction and biomarker discovery** from omics and clinical data
- **ML/DL and generative AI (LLMs)** for identifying and optimising lead drug compounds
- **AI for faster clinical trials** and personalised medicines with higher efficacy and fewer side effects

### Why drug discovery?

Most drug candidates fail, often because the biology behind a target wasn't understood early enough. As a pharmacist, I've seen what slow, costly development means for patients. My long-term goal is **AI engineering in pharmaceutical R&D**: using AI to make drug discovery faster, smarter and more personalised.
